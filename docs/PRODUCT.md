# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

People choosing what to wear to a particular occasion or event and looking for quick, practical feedback on how their outfit fits the event's theme.

## Product Purpose

Help a person evaluate an outfit for a chosen occasion by submitting a photo and receiving a score plus actionable styling feedback.

## Positioning

Candidate positioning, based on the user's description: occasion-specific outfit feedback that pairs a one-decimal score with practical suggestions for what to improve next time. Market differentiation has not been researched or validated.

## Operating Context

The user identifies the event or theme, takes or selects an outfit photo, reviews a score and explanation, and can use the advice when choosing a future outfit. The user also wants a small rotatable, fidgetable icon or object in the center of the app experience, and the outfit shown on a friendly, cartoon-style 3D avatar that can be spun through multiple angles. The user wants the avatar to look like a Snapchat Bitmoji.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The planned rating is out of 10 and displayed with one digit after the decimal point.
- Feedback should identify what worked, what fit the theme less well, and what the person could do differently next time.
- A responsive web app is also requested and should match the iOS experience as closely as the web platform allows.
- The outfit should be shown on a rotatable, expressive cartoon-style 3D avatar with direct manipulation and controls for viewing multiple angles. The avatar should use an original generic design rather than copy an existing character brand; a Bitmoji-like look is the target style, built from original artwork. How a 3D avatar and outfit representation are produced from a photo, how accurately they represent clothing, and what customization to offer remain open technical and product decisions.
- Monetization, rating methodology, image handling, account requirements, and whether results are saved are undecided.
- The repository name is `suitup`; whether this is the confirmed customer-facing product name remains to be confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

The product concept and requested behavior are based on the user's description. No user research, market research, product analytics, customer evidence, brand assets, or validated competitive analysis has been provided. Do not present synthetic examples or market claims as factual.

## Product Principles

- Make feedback specific to the stated occasion or theme.
- Pair the score with understandable reasons and practical next-time suggestions.
- Keep the experience centered on the outfit and styling choices.
- Treat the rotatable fidget element as an optional interaction, not a substitute for the core task.
