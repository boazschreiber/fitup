# Lessons Learned

This is the durable record of approved, reusable lessons from project work. It is not a live incident tracker. The Scribe reports candidate incidents to the Observer; the Observer relays them to the Orchestrator; only the Orchestrator decides whether an incident belongs here.

Do not add sensitive or identifying information. Record the behavior and prevention, not blame. Keep entries concise, evidence-based, and actionable. A lesson does not itself authorize a charter edit: after recording one, the Orchestrator must assess all charters and ask the human to approve any specific proposed updates before changing them.

### Entry template

Use this template for each accepted lesson:

#### YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

- **Context:** Task or workflow involved; omit unnecessary identifying details.
- **What happened:** Observable behavior and evidence.
- **Impact:** User, project, quality, or operational impact.
- **Lesson:** Reusable principle supported by the incident.
- **Prevention:** Specific practice that could prevent recurrence.
- **Charters reviewed:** Roles considered and any proposed updates; record approval outcome separately from the lesson.
